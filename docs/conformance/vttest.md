# vttest screens: owner checklist

Generated by `Harf --conformance <dir>` (a picture and a text dump of every screen; this folder keeps the index, all text dumps and the pictures I looked at. Re-create all 93 pictures with `macos/.build/Harf.app/Contents/MacOS/Harf --conformance <dir>`, about 7 minutes).
Each screen of `vttest` says on itself what a correct display is. **The "Owner" column is yours; I have not marked any screen right on your behalf.** The "Observed" column is what I saw: the pictures I read are in `pictures/`, otherwise I read the text dump, and screens I did not examine say so.

## Findings (Harf behavior, for you to rule on)

- **F1. 132-column mode (DECCOLM) — Fixed after capture.** At the time these screenshots were taken, vttest switched the terminal to 132 columns while the view stayed 80 wide (clipping the right 52 columns). Fixed later via `followProgramGrid` (`ARCHITECTURE.md` 2026-11-14, `docs/phase3-done.md`), which resizes the window/view to fit DECCOLM 80/132. The captured screenshots in `docs/conformance/` predate this fix.
- **F2. DECREQTPARM (terminal parameters report) is not answered** (menu 6.7). This is the library; the sequence is VT100-era and rarely used.
- **F3. Blink is drawn steady** because the terminal has no timers (slice 3 decision).
- **F4. The DEC "alternate character ROM" sets 1 and 2** (menu 3.8) show plain ASCII; they are VT100 hardware features.
- **Not walked by the script:** 7 screens need a human (menu 3.4 to 3.7 ask you to choose a character set; menu 3.13 and menu 11.1.7 need typed keys), and 151 menu paths deeper than three levels in menu 11 are listed in the index as not walked: run `vttest` by hand and choose that path.
